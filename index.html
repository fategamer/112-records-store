ABOUT · THE MOVEMENT

11/2 is not a trend.
It is a standard.

Eleven over Two Records (11/2) is the official label and culture arm behind FATE GAMER. Born from 33, Hola, Kenya — and built to move far beyond it.

Discipline Over Drama is the operating system. Faith. Focus. Consistency. Purpose. The same principles that shape the music also shape every piece of merch that leaves this store.

This is not fast fashion. It is wearable identity for people who choose the long game: artists, builders, and culture carriers who refuse noise without substance.

From the studio to the street, 11/2 exists so the sound has a physical standard — premium cuts, metallic prints, true street fit — designed to be worn, not left in the cart.
