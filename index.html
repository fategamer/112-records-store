Shipping & care

Worldwide orders

Orders process through official Shopify checkout. Based in Kenya — shipping available to international customers. Delivery times vary by destination after dispatch.

Sizing

Relaxed street fit. Most customers take true size. Prefer oversized? Size up one. Available S, M, L, XL, XXL on every piece.

Care

Wash inside-out cold. Hang dry preferred. Low heat only if needed. Do not iron directly on the print. Protect the metallic finish.

Secure payment

All purchases complete on Shopify’s secure checkout. Your card and order data never pass through this marketing site.

FAQ

Before you order

Is this the official 11/2 Records store?

Yes. This is the official merch storefront for 11/2 Records / DOD. Checkout is completed on the connected Shopify store.

How do I buy?

Open any product → choose size → complete payment on Shopify. You can also use “Buy now” from the product drawer on this site.

What currency is pricing in?

Prices are listed in Kenyan Shillings (KES). Shopify may show conversion depending on your location and payment method.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — worldwide orders are welcome. Shipping options and costs appear at Shopify checkout based on destination.

Where is the artist site?

Music, story, and bookings live at fategamer.com. This site is dedicated to the label store only.
